Common questions

How are Mooligai powders made? add

Every batch is washed, dried under open sun for up to five days, then stone-ground in small quantities so the natural oils stay intact. Nothing is added.

Do your products contain fillers or synthetic fragrance? add

No. We don't add fillers, silicones or synthetic fragrance.

How should I store the powders and oil? add

Keep them in a tightly closed container in a cool, dry place, and always use a dry spoon so moisture doesn't get in.

Should I do a patch test first? add

Yes. As with any herbal product, try a small amount on your inner arm and wait a day before using it on your face or scalp.
